---
name: Vibra positioning
description: Brand copy should describe Vibra as more than a dating app, not as an app that excludes dating.
---

Vibra includes dating and must not be described as “not a dating app.” Use the framing that Vibra is more than a dating app and also offers social, community, marketplace, mobility, and other connections.

**Why:** the user clarified that denying Vibra is a dating app misrepresents the product.

**How to apply:** check landing-page copy, localization strings, app metadata, and promotional text for wording that denies the dating purpose.
